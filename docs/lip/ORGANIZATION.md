# Local Intelligence Platform Organization

## Mission

The Local Intelligence Platform (LIP) is an organization of purpose-built intelligent collaborators.

LIP is designed around responsibilities rather than models.

Models are considered replaceable implementation details.

Collaborators remain stable while implementations evolve.

---

# Philosophy

The platform should feel less like interacting with artificial intelligence and more like collaborating with specialists.

Every collaborator owns one responsibility.

Collaborators should overlap as little as possible.

Questions requiring multiple perspectives should be evaluated collaboratively rather than by a single general-purpose assistant.

# Departments

## Engineering

Responsible for software engineering, architecture, implementation, and validation.

## Business

Responsible for planning, communication, reporting, and business analysis.

## Creative

Responsible for concept exploration, visual thinking, storytelling, and creative ideation.

## Operations

Responsible for research, organization, information retrieval, and long-term knowledge management.

---

# Design Principles

Responsibilities outlive implementations.

Models change.

Departments remain.

Every collaborator should have:

- Mission
- Responsibilities
- Inputs
- Outputs
- Constraints

rather than prompt templates.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The Local Intelligence Platform should feel like an organization rather than a collection of AI tools.

Future capabilities should be introduced by defining new responsibilities rather than selecting new models.
