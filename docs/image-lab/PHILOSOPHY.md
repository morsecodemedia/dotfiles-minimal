mage Lab Philosophy

Image Lab exists to support human creativity.

Artificial intelligence accelerates creative work, but it does not replace
creative direction, artistic judgment, or human intent.

The platform is designed around long-lived creative assets rather than isolated
image generation.

---

## Visual Thinking Before Visual Generation

Generation is only one step of the creative process.

Research, references, composition, iteration, and evaluation are equally
important.

The platform should support the complete creative workflow.

---

## References Over Prompts

Humans communicate visually.

Reference images, style guides, color palettes, sketches, and mood boards
communicate creative intent more effectively than prompts alone.

References are first-class creative assets.

---

## Identities Over Images

Images are outputs.

Identities persist.

Characters, locations, products, brands, creatures, materials, and styles should
remain reusable across projects while continuing to evolve over time.

---

## Projects Over Folders

Creative work belongs to projects.

Projects provide context for references, workflows, outputs, and publications.

Folders organize files.

Projects organize ideas.

---

## Creativity Is Iterative

Creative work improves through exploration.

The platform should encourage experimentation while preserving successful ideas
through versioning and reusable identities.

---

## Local-First Ownership

Creative work belongs to its creator.

Projects, references, generated assets, and supporting resources should remain
available without dependence on cloud services.

---

## Human Creativity Remains the Director

Image Lab assists the creative process.

It does not replace the creator.

Creative direction, artistic judgment, and final decisions always belong to the
human.
